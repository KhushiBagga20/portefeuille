This is a sample, not a poem of mine.
It is here so the room has something to hold
while the real ones are on their way.

Line breaks stay exactly
    where you put them,
and so do indents.

A blank line starts a new stanza.
Nothing else is needed: no symbols, no tags,
just the lines, in the order you want them read.

To replace it, open this file
(src/content/writing/sample-poem.md),
keep the few lines at the very top,
and write over everything underneath.
